Forge

Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Forge ("we") is a training coach app for iPhone, Apple Watch and Android. This policy explains what Forge collects, where it goes, and the choices you have. Short version: your data is yours. We use it only to run Forge for you. We never sell it, never use it for advertising, and never share it with anyone you haven't chosen.

What Forge keeps

  • Your account: your email address, and your name if you sign in with Apple or Google. If you choose "Hide My Email" with Apple, we only see Apple's relay address.
  • Your profile and goals: name, age, sex, height, bodyweight, training level, goals and the reason you train.
  • Training: workouts and sets, routines, your weekly plan, activities, interval sessions and results, and the coach's notes on them.
  • Body and food: weigh-ins (with the factors you log, like hydration), daily calorie and protein totals, check-ins (how you feel), injuries, and progress photos.
  • Daily health totals: steps, active and total energy burned, each night's sleep (times and stages), and your daily readiness score with the resting heart rate and heart-rate variability it was based on.

Some of this comes from Apple Health, Health Connect or your watch, and only with your permission, which you can withdraw at any time in your phone's settings. Forge does not upload raw heart-rate samples. Those stay on your phone.

Where it's stored

Everything above is kept on your phone. When you're signed in, it's also backed up to your Forge account, so you can change phones without losing your history. The account is hosted by Supabase in Sydney, Australia, and encrypted in transit and at rest. Progress photos go to private storage that only you can open, unless you share them (see below). You can turn photo backup off in Settings → Account. That removes your photos from the account and keeps them on your phone.

Forge never writes backed-up data into Apple Health or Health Connect. Data restored from your account stays inside Forge.

Who can see it

  • You.
  • Your personal trainer, only if you link one. When you link a trainer, you choose which categories they can see (training, weight, check-ins, food, sleep, readiness, photos). You can change those or end the link at any time, and access stops straight away.
  • AI coach providers, only if you set one up. If you add your own key for an AI provider (for example Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic or Groq), Forge sends the training and health details a coaching answer needs to that provider, using your key. Their own privacy policy applies to that request. Without a key, nothing is sent.
  • Services you connect. If you connect MyFitnessPal, Hevy or a calendar, Forge reads from them with the details you give it.

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don't sell or rent your data. We'd only disclose it if the law required us to.

Your choices

  • Sign out: Forge finishes backing up, then removes your data from that phone.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete account permanently deletes your account and everything stored with it, including photos. It can't be undone.
  • See or correct your data: everything Forge keeps is visible and editable in the app. For anything else, email us.

Health information

Health and fitness information is sensitive, and we treat it that way. We collect it only to coach you, and use it for nothing else. Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising and never shared with third parties for their own purposes. Forge is a coaching tool, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything.

Children

Forge is for people aged 16 and over.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app. For questions, access requests or complaints, email forge.coach.fitness@gmail.com. If you're in Australia and not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
