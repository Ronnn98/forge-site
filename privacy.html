Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Who we are

Forge is operated by Charisma Holdings NSW Pty Limited (ABN 40 612 588 824). We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. Your data is yours: we use it only to run Forge for you, never sell it and never use it for advertising. Privacy contact: forge.coach.fitness@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your account: your email address, and your name if you sign in with Apple or Google. Your profile and goals: name, age, sex, height, bodyweight, training level, goals and the reason you train. Training: workouts, routines, your plan, activities, interval sessions and the coach's notes. Body and food: weigh-ins, daily calorie and protein totals, check-ins, injuries, progress photos and your answers to the health questions. Daily health totals from Apple Health, Health Connect or your watch, with your permission: steps, energy burned, sleep, and your readiness score with the resting heart rate and heart-rate variability behind it. Raw heart-rate samples stay on your phone. Location during an interval session, used for pace and distance: we keep the distance, not your route. Health information is sensitive information, and we collect it only with your consent.

Why we collect it

To coach you: to set targets, build plans, adjust for injuries and health answers, show your progress, back up your data across your devices and keep the service secure. Nothing else.

Where it's stored

On your phone and, when you're signed in, in your Forge account, hosted by Supabase in Sydney, Australia, and encrypted in transit and at rest. Progress photos go to private storage only you can open unless you share them, and you can turn photo backup off in Settings → Account. Sign-in tokens are kept in your phone's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure, and we'll tell you about a data breach where the law requires it.

Who can see it

You. A personal trainer, only if you link one: you choose which categories they see and can end the link at any time. An AI provider, only if you add your own key for one (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic or Groq): Forge then sends your message with the details a coaching answer needs, which can include your first name, weight, height, age, sex, goals, health answers and recent training and food. Without a key, nothing is sent. Services you connect, such as MyFitnessPal, Hevy or a calendar. The sign-in provider you use. Authorities, where the law requires it. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Overseas disclosure

AI providers and sign-in providers process information outside Australia, mainly in the United States. By adding an AI key you consent to that. Those providers may not be bound by Australian privacy law, and their own privacy policies apply.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your data while your account exists. Signing out finishes the backup, then removes your data from that phone. Deleting your account in Settings → Account permanently removes your account, its backed-up records and your progress photos from our servers. It can't be undone.

Your choices

Everything Forge keeps is visible and editable in the app. You can withdraw a health permission in your phone's settings, disconnect a service, stop sharing with a trainer, and withdraw consent by deleting your account. Email us for a copy of your data or to have it corrected.

Children

Forge is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

Changes and complaints

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app. Email us with questions or complaints. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au. This policy is also published at https://ronnn98.github.io/forge-site/privacy.html.

See also the Terms of Use.
